## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Death Metal vs Rap - A dark comedy adventure about a Swedish death metal band on a journey to destroy the supernatural force of rap and save heavy metal.

**Storyline:**

The game follows a Swedish death metal band called In Flames as they travel through a dark and chaotic music world on a quest to destroy the Rap King and restore heavy metal. The player must explore different locations and collect six powerful metal artifacts a ESP guitar, Spiked microphone, Drumstick of Boom, Ancient vinyl, Battle vest, and Skull amplifier. After collecting all six items, the band can enter the Rap King's final arena and defeat him. If the player enters the final arena before collecting every item, the Rap King wins and takes over the music world.

## Items

1. ESP Guitar
2. Spiked Microphone
3. Drumsticks of Doom
4. Ancient Vinyl
5. Battle Vest
6. Skull Amplifier


If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

The Rap King's is the final villain who wants to take over the music world and wipe out heavy metal. The player must face him after collecting all six metal artifacts.
